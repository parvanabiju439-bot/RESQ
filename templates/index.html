How RESQ works

Prototype
🧑‍🚒 Dynamic Help Radius
Starts at 800 m and expands in 800 m steps until enough available responders are found, up to the 5 km demo limit.
🤖 AI-style Emergency Classification
Rule-based, transparent keyword classification of your words into category, reported condition and priority. It is not a medical diagnosis.
🚑 Smart Route Calculation
Victim → responder and victim → open hospital ETAs are shown. The demo uses simulated traffic; optional OSRM routing can be enabled.
📞 Instant Control-Station Callback
When Twilio is configured, RESQ immediately requests an automated phone call to the number entered above after an SOS is created.
